# Career 2026: Settling Scores and a 2026 Check (V1.4): Design Spec

Oct 31, 2026 · @Dimitris · Movie Prediction App · **V1.4** (after V1.3, the one movies database)

## Goal

The 1,199 films of 2026 are already in `movies.db`, and the Career section already uses them. This change keeps the track-record forecast honest with them and turns them into fresh proof that the forecast works:

1. **Settling rule (A):** a film's score counts only once it was 182 days old when we pulled it. Younger films stay on the chart, marked as settling.
2. **2026 check (B):** every person is forecast from their films up to 2025 only, and their later films are checked against that range. The page shows the role's 2026 hit rate and, for this person, how their later films did.

**Success:** a young, fan-inflated score no longer moves a forecast; the explanation carries a 2026 hit rate measured on films the forecast never saw; a page like Nolan's says "From films up to 2025 we said 6.6–8.3. *The Odyssey* scored 8.0 so far (still settling), inside."

**Constraints carried over:** fast and simple; nothing is re-tuned; h, k, q, the score model and the 2019+ backtest numbers stay exactly as they are; Day 9 memory gate (< ~450 MB); every page carries TMDB attribution (unchanged).

## Why

- **Young scores run high.** 2026 films with 1,000+ votes average 7.52, against 6.93 for 2025: early votes come from fans. Only 202 of the 410 rated 2026 films were 6+ months old when pulled.
- **The model already has this rule:** an earlier film's score counts only once it is 182 days old (`INCOMPLETE_DAYS` in `ml/data/clean.py`). The career section didn't.
- **The age is measured at pull time**, not today: the stored score is a snapshot and changes only when the film is pulled again.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Settled | `pulled_at` date − `release_date` ≥ 182 days (`SETTLE_DAYS = INCOMPLETE_DAYS`); no release date or no `pulled_at` → settled |
| Settling film | Drawn on the chart (dashed outline, tooltip "Score still settling"); left out of the career line, the best film and the forecast; still used by the model recipes (genres and collaborators, not scores) |
| Cut year for the check | `MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR` (2025): "up to 2025" vs "after 2025" |
| Role hit rate | Built by `make career` into `career_forecast.json` (`holdout` per role), settled later films only, frozen settings, nothing tuned |
| Per-person check | Computed per request in `/career`; settling films shown with a label but never counted in the role hit rate |
| Where it shows | One sentence in "How is this calculated?" plus a line on the track-record card |
| Name | V1.4 |

## 1. Settling rule

### `backend/app/repo/career.py`

- `SETTLE_DAYS = INCOMPLETE_DAYS` (imported from `ml.data.clean`).
- `career_films` also selects `pulled_at`, and each `CareerFilm` gets `settling: bool`:
  `settling = release_date and pulled_at and (date(pulled_at) − date(release_date)).days < SETTLE_DAYS`.
- `rated` keeps its meaning (50+ votes and a score). A film counts for the line, best film and forecast when `rated and not settling` ("counted").
- `RATED` (SQL, used by roles, population stats, recipes and the backtest) is unchanged. `roles_available`'s `rated_count` is unchanged.
- **Today only 2026 films are settling:** every film up to 2025 was 182+ days old when pulled (checked: 0 exceptions). So the backtest's tuned numbers don't move; a test pins that.

### `backend/app/services/career/forecast.py`

- `career_response` uses counted films for `line`, `best_film` and `forecast`.
- Each film in the response carries `settling`.

## 2. The 2026 check

### Role hit rate: `ml/career/backtest.py`

- **Points:** for each person and role, every rated, settled film with `year > MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR`, when the person has 5+ rated films up to `MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR`.
- **History:** their rated films up to `MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR` only (not earlier 2026 films). All of them are settled.
- **Forecast:** `track_record` with the role's tuned settings and the role's `mu`, `sigma_pop` as written to the artifact (the 10 years up to 2025), the same numbers the API uses.
- **Weights:** each person's points share a total weight of 1, as in the main backtest.
- **Output:** `roles.<role>.holdout = { year_from, coverage, mae, baseline_mae, n_people, n_points }`. `year_from` = `MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR + 1`. With no points: `coverage`, `mae`, `baseline_mae` = `null`, counts 0.
- **Artifact version 2.** `load_career_artifact` requires `holdout` with every field; a missing one names the file and `make career`, as today.
- **Nothing is tuned on it.** h, k, q come only from the pre-2019 tuning, as before.
- The CLI's per-role table gets the holdout columns.

Expected sizes on today's database: acting ~935 points, directing ~58, writing ~74.

### Per person: `/career`'s new `check` field

```
check: {
  low, mid, high,                      // their forecast from films up to 2025 only
  films: [{ movie_id, title, year, score, settling, inside }]   // their rated films after 2025, oldest first
} | null
```

- Range from `track_record` over their counted films up to `MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR`, same settings and population numbers as `forecast`.
- `films`: their rated films with `year > MODEL_DATA_MAX_YEAR` (settling ones included); `inside` = `low ≤ score ≤ high`.
- `null` when they have fewer than 5 counted films up to 2025, or no rated film after 2025.
- `forecast.test` gains `holdout: { coverage, n_points }` from the artifact.
- No cache: one `track_record` call, microseconds.

## 3. Frontend (`frontend/src/career/`)

- **Chart (`CareerChart.tsx`):** a settling film is a dashed-outline dot (`data-testid="dot-settling"`), drawn whatever its lead/supporting style; its tooltip adds "Score still settling". It is not part of the line, so the line ends at the last counted film. The screen-reader summary counts counted films only.
- **Explanation (`explain.ts`):** one sentence after the 2019 sentence, from `forecast.test.holdout`:
  > We also checked films from 2026, which the forecast never saw: 8 out of 10 of 935 landed inside.
  - "N out of 10" = coverage rounded to the nearest 10%, as the 2019 sentence.
  - Fewer than 100 points: add " (a small sample)" before the full stop.
  - No points (`coverage` null): no sentence.
- **Track-record card (`TrackRecordCard.tsx`):** when `check` is not null, a line under the range:
  > From films up to 2025 we said 6.6–8.3. *The Odyssey* scored 8.0 so far (still settling), inside.
  - One sentence per film after the first: "*Film* scored 7.1, outside."
  - "so far (still settling)" only for settling films; the title links to `/movies/:id`.
  - Scores to 1 decimal with the existing `points()` helper.
- **Types:** `make gen-api` after the schema change. Fixtures `src/test/career_*.json` re-saved from the real API (Nolan for the check line, Hanks for the plain case).

## 4. States

| Situation | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| No film after 2025 | No check line |
| Fewer than 5 counted films up to 2025 | No check line (the main forecast may still show) |
| Only settling films after 2025 | Check line, every film labelled "so far (still settling)" |
| Role with no 2026 points | No 2026 sentence in the explanation |
| Role with < 100 points | 2026 sentence ends "(a small sample)" |

## 5. Testing

**Python**
- Settled boundary: 181 days → settling, 182 → settled; no release date → settled; no `pulled_at` → settled.
- Settling films are in `films` with `settling: true` and left out of `line`, `best_film` and the forecast's `n_rated`.
- Backtest:
  - holdout points use only history up to 2025 (time travel: changing 2026 scores changes no history);
  - equal person weights;
  - with the fixture, the tuned h, k, q and 2019+ `test` numbers equal the run without the holdout;
  - artifact v2 has every `holdout` field; a missing field fails loading.
- `/career`: `check` present with films and `inside` flags; null for fewer than 5 counted films up to 2025 and for no later film; `forecast.test.holdout` present; schemas match.

**Frontend (Vitest)**
- `dot-settling` drawn for a settling film; tooltip text.
- Explanation: the 2026 sentence, the small-sample suffix, no sentence without points.
- Track-record card: the check line with "inside" / "outside", the settling label, the film link; no line when `check` is null.

**Smoke and manual**
- `scripts/smoke.py`: Nolan's `/career?role=directing` has a `check` with *The Odyssey*.
- Docker: memory after 100 predictions and 50 career pages stays under the gate.
- By hand: Nolan (check line, dashed dot), Hanks, a person with no 2026 film; phone and desktop, both themes.

## Cut lines (in order)

1. Per-film links in the check line → plain titles.
2. The "(a small sample)" suffix.

Never cut: the settling rule, the role's 2026 hit rate in the artifact, the explanation sentence, the per-person check line.

## Out of scope

Re-tuning h, k, q with 2026 films; using 2026 films in the score model or its track-record features; re-pulling 2026 films to refresh their scores; a check for the model films ("Their usual film" / "Something new"); counting live-refreshed (fresh) films.

## Done when

On a person page:
- settling films show as dashed dots and don't move the line or forecast;
- the explanation carries the 2026 hit rate from the artifact;
- people with films after 2025 see the check line.

All tests above pass, smoke passes, and memory stays under the gate.
